What it does

  • Snapshots all open windows and tabs — manually, automatically, or at browser startup.
  • Restores a session in three modes: replace the current window, open in a new window, or merge into the current window.
  • Restores tab groups (title, color, collapsed state) and pinned tabs.
  • Keeps a log of recently closed tabs, so an accidentally closed tab is one click away.
  • Searches saved sessions and imports your own Session Buddy / OneTab JSON exports.

Local-first

SessionSnap stores everything in your browser’s local storage. It makes zero network requests and has no account, no tracking, and no uploads. Browser-internal tabs (chrome://, about:, extension pages) cannot be re-opened by an extension, so they are left out of snapshots.
